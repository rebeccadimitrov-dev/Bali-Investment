Questions Aussies ask me first

Can Australians invest in Bali property?+

Yes, absolutely, and it's simpler than most people think. You can hold a leasehold in your own name, or invest through your own Indonesian company (called a PT PMA), which is ideal for running a rental business. Our in-house legal team sets up the right structure for you, so it's secure and stress-free from day one.

Is it only villas?+

Not at all. Through my developer network you can invest in villas, apartments, studios and hotel rooms. Smaller options like studios and apartments are a great way to start, while villas and hotels suit investors looking to scale.

Do I need to move to Bali?+

No. Most people stay in Australia and have the property managed on the ground. Some fall in love with it anyway.

How much do I need to start?+

Less than most people expect. Properties start from around $100K, and with off-plan you can pay in instalments while it's built, so you don't need the full amount up front.

What if I don't have the full amount saved?+

That's exactly what off-plan projects are great for. You secure the property with a deposit, then pay the rest in stages while it's being built. It means you can get started with the savings you have now, and keep paying from your income as it's built.

How do the instalment payment plans work?+

Each developer sets their own plan, but it usually looks like a deposit to secure your property, then payments spread across the build as each stage is completed. I'll walk you through the exact terms for any project you're interested in.

Who looks after my property when I'm not there?+

A local management team handles everything: bookings, guests, cleaning and maintenance. You get regular reporting on how your property is performing, so you can stay hands-off from Australia.

Can I stay in it myself?+

Yes. You can block out dates for your own holidays. Just keep in mind that every night you stay is a night it isn't earning, so most owners plan their stays around the quieter seasons.

How do I get paid?+

Once your property is running, the management team pays out your share of the rental income regularly, with a statement showing bookings, costs and what you've earned. The exact schedule depends on the property, and I'll walk you through it before you commit.

When does my property start earning?+

A ready-built property can start taking bookings almost straight away. With off-plan, it starts earning once the build is finished and handed over. I'll show you the expected timeline for any project you're looking at.

Can I sell it later?+

Yes. You can sell the remaining lease, or the company that holds the property, whenever it suits you. Many investors hold for the income, then sell and move into their next property.

Do you help with the legal side?+

Yes. We have an in-house legal team to guide you through every step, from choosing the right ownership structure to contracts, permits and title checks. Whatever legal support you need, we've got you covered, so you're never navigating it alone.
